👑 ⚓ Kings of Watershallow

Agents, adventurers and ambition in a city of shifting tides.

How to play / implementation notes
Eight rounds. Assign one agent to an empty space, resolve its action, then optionally complete one owned commission. Harbour spaces require an Order and let that agent act again during harbour reassignment. Buy buildings at the Foundry; other players can visit them and pay the owner a benefit. Claim the Council to go first next round. Round five adds an agent. Final scoring adds one point per adventurer, one per two coins, and your secret agenda bonus. Public quest cards must first be acquired at the Guild.

This is an original implementation of a worker-placement rules structure, not an official digital edition. City layout, names, quest decks, buildings, Orders and agenda combinations are original; deck balance and some card effects differ. No commercial artwork or rulebook text is included.
