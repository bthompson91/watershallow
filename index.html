👑 ⚓ Kings of Watershallow

Agents, adventurers and ambition in a city of shifting tides.

How to play / implementation notes
Click an empty agent space directly on a city building. Resolve the action, optionally complete one owned commission, then end your turn. Harbour agents act again after ordinary placement ends. Buy buildings at the Foundry; they appear in the South Quay district on the same map. Other players visiting a purchased building give its owner a benefit. The Council claims next round's starting player. Round five adds an agent. Final scoring adds one point per adventurer, one per two coins, and your secret agenda bonus.

Original city, cards and presentation. Not an official digital edition. Deck balance and some effects differ from other worker-placement games. MIT-licensed code. V2 saves remain supported; old prototype saves are not imported.
